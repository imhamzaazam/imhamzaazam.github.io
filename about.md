# Hi, I'm Hamza.

I'm a computer science graduate. I've always been curious about how things work, especially startups, fintech, and technology. I also have a habit of breaking things just to see what happens.

I've worked with fintech startups like [Tweeq](https://tweeq.sa/), [Tabby](https://tabby.ai/), and [D Bank](https://din.global/), spending most of my time around digital wallets, lending, money transfers, and the business of moving money.

These days, I'm building **Stealth Startup**. Still figuring things out, making mistakes, and hopefully getting a few things right along the way.

## Interests & curiosities

Banking, economics, power structures, and cybersecurity. Basically, everything that makes the world run — and everything that could bring it down.

I'm interested in how the internet works, how systems break, who's really in control, and why privacy seems to be getting harder to come by. The darker side of technology, vulnerabilities, surveillance, and the power structures behind it all fascinate me.

## Things I think should exist

Less bureaucracy, more freedom, better systems, and technology that actually serves people.

## Outside the terminal

Usually swimming, travelling, exploring somewhere new, or looking for something good to eat.

I'm always up for a good conversation, an interesting idea, or a cup of chai.
